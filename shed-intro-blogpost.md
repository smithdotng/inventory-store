# Introducing Shed: The Smarter Way to Run Your Business

Running a business is hard enough without your tools making it harder. Between tracking stock, sending invoices, following up on orders, and keeping customers happy — most small business owners are juggling too many things at once, often across too many apps, notebooks, or mental notes.

That's exactly why we built Shed.

---

## What Is Shed?

Shed is a business management platform built for entrepreneurs who want to sell anywhere and manage everything from one place. The name says it all: think of a well-stocked shed — everything you need, exactly where you expect it, ready when you are.

Whether you run a retail shop, sell online, manage wholesale orders, or do all three, Shed gives you the infrastructure to do it cleanly and confidently.

---

## What Shed Does for You

### 1. Inventory That Always Knows Where It Stands

One of the biggest silent killers in small business is inventory confusion — selling what you don't have, or sitting on stock you forgot about. Shed gives you a live view of everything you carry, so you always know what's in, what's running low, and what needs restocking. No spreadsheets, no guessing.

### 2. Your Own Online Storefront

Every Shed account comes with a ready-to-go storefront — a clean, professional page where customers can browse your products, add to cart, and place orders directly. No coding needed, no third-party marketplace fees eating into your margins. Just your business, presented properly.

### 3. Invoicing and Orders, Simplified

Chasing payments is exhausting. Shed lets you generate and send invoices in seconds, track what's been paid and what's outstanding, and keep your order history organized — all in one place. Your customers get a better experience, and you get your money faster.

### 4. Sell Through WhatsApp and Beyond

Nigerian business runs on WhatsApp. Shed is built with that in mind — sharing your storefront link or product details through WhatsApp is seamless, so your customers can shop the way they already prefer to communicate.

### 5. Everything in One Dashboard

No more switching between five different apps for five different functions. Shed's dashboard brings your inventory, orders, customers, and store performance together so you can make decisions quickly and keep moving.
